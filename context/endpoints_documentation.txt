  - "scope_3_cat4"          Category 4: Upstream Transportation and Distribution

  - "scope_3_cat6"          Category 6: Business Travel
  - "scope_3_cat7"          Category 7: Employee Commuting
  - "scope_3_cat9"          Category 9: Downstream Transportation and Distribution
  - "scope_3_cat15"         Category 15: Investments & Financed Emissions
  - "brsr"                  BRSR Reports & Disclosures
  - "cbam"                  EU CBAM Setup, Calculations & Excel Exporter
  - "eia"                   Industrial Stack Air Emissions Monitoring
  - "facilities"            Facility, Contacts, and Meter Management
  - "reporting_periods"     Reporting Accounting Periods Lifecycle
  - "analytics"             GHG Protocols & Energy Consumption Analytics
  - "team_members"          Tenant Team Member & User Administration
  - "audit_trails"          Compliance & Activity Audit Logs

Access Level Hierarchy (Total Ordering):
  NONE ("none", rank 0)   <   READ ("read", rank 10)   <   WRITE ("write", rank 20)   <   ADMIN ("admin", rank 30)

Role Default Permissions Matrix:
  - TENANT_OWNER:  ADMIN across all 19 components
  - TENANT_ADMIN:  ADMIN across all 19 components
  - ESG_TEAM:      WRITE across Scopes 1, 2, 3, BRSR, CBAM, EIA, Facilities, Periods, Analytics; READ on Audit Trails; NONE on Team Members
  - FACILITY_HEAD: WRITE on Scope 1, Scope 2, Facilities; READ on Scope 3, BRSR, Periods, Analytics; NONE on Team Members & Audit Trails
  - AUDITOR:       READ across all operational components and Audit Trails; NONE on Team Members
