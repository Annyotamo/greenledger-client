1. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK & PROTOCOL ISSUE 7.X OVERVIEW
================================================================================

1.1 The Leather Working Group (LWG) Standard
--------------------------------------------
The Leather Working Group (LWG) Environmental Audit Protocol is the global
benchmark for environmental, chemical, social, and operational compliance across
leather manufacturing facilities. Protocol Issue 7.x establishes rigorous
quantitative thresholds for resource efficiency, hazardous waste containment,
air/effluent discharges, and full traceability across supply tiers.

1.2 Certification Medal Tiers & Scoring Benchmarks
--------------------------------------------------
Upon completion of an official on-site audit, tanneries are classified into five
standardized certification tiers based on percentage scores:

  Tier             Minimum Score Threshold   Protocol Criteria
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GOLD MEDAL       >= 85.0%                  Exemplary world-class sustainability
  SILVER MEDAL     >= 75.0%                  Strong environmental performance
  BRONZE MEDAL     >= 65.0%                  Compliant baseline ESG performance
  AUDITED          >= 50.0%                  Audited without meeting medal cutoff
  FAIL / NON-COMPL < 50.0%                   Non-compliant; accreditation withheld

1.3 The 24-Month Sliding Audit Window (P12 vs L12)
--------------------------------------------------
Per LWG Issue 7.x rules, an environmental audit requires 24 continuous calendar
months of contiguous operational data. The system automatically partitions this
24-month audit window into two distinct 12-month periods:

  1. Previous 12 Months (P12: Months 1 to 12):
     Acts as the historical facility baseline. Used to establish consumption
     benchmarks for energy, water, and raw material throughput.
  2. Latest 12 Months (L12: Months 13 to 24):
     Acts as the active audit evaluation period. All final intensity calculations,
     specific consumption figures, and Year-over-Year (YoY) trajectories are
     judged based on L12 performance relative to P12.

*Note for ESG Analysts*: Every monthly operational entry must fall strictly on the
first day of the calendar month ("YYYY-MM-01") within `[data_window_start, data_window_end]`.
Dates outside this boundary are rejected by protocol rules.

1.4 Core Protocol Mathematical Rules & Denominators
---------------------------------------------------
In leather environmental auditing, intensities are normalized against leather
surface area. GreenLedger continuously computes and stores dual units:
  - Metric Standard      : Square Meters (m²)
  - Asian Trade Standard : Square Feet (sq ft)
  - Official Conversion  : 1 m² = 10.7639 sq ft (1 sq ft = 0.092903 m²)

Denominators for Facility-Wide Intensity:
When computing facility-wide Specific Energy Consumption (MJ/m²) and Specific
Water Intake (L/m²), the production denominator is derived from the highest
completed manufacturing stage operated on-site:
  - Stage 3 (Crust to Finish) : Denominator is finished leather area.
  - Stage 2 (Wet Blue to Crust): Denominator is crust leather area.
  - Stage 1 (Raw to Wet Blue)  : Denominator is wet blue leather area.

1.5 The LWG "Holding-Down Rule" (Critical Section Principle)
-----------------------------------------------------------
The single most vital principle an ESG analyst must understand is the
"Holding-Down Rule" (Protocol Issue 7 Section 9.3):

  "The overall facility medal rating cannot exceed the score achieved in any
   single Critical Section."

Example Scenario:
A facility achieves a 92% score (Gold) in Section 4 (Permits), 89% (Gold) in
Section 5 (Production), and 88% (Gold) in Section 10 (Energy). However, in
Section 11 (Water Usage), the facility achieves 72% (Bronze).
Even though the facility's mathematical weighted average across all sections
exceeds 86% (Gold), the Holding-Down Rule forces the overall tannery rating to
be CAPPED AT BRONZE. Section 11 acts as a "Holding-Down Bottleneck."
GreenLedger's engine automatically highlights this bottleneck in red/amber alerts
across the Dashboard and Simulator.

=========================================
LWG Protocol Issue 7.x comprises 17 sections, categorized into Critical (must meet

  Sec 2     | Traceability: Subcontracted Operations       | Critical     | Full Scope
  Sec 3     | Social Audit Compliance & Labor Standards    | Non-Critical | Full Scope
  Sec 4     | Operating Permits & Environmental Approvals  | Critical     | ACTIVE MVP
  Sec 5     | Monthly Production Data Throughput           | Critical     | ACTIVE MVP
  Sec 6     | Incoming Raw Material Traceability           | Non-Critical | Full Scope
  Sec 7     | Outgoing Finished Product Traceability       | Non-Critical | Full Scope
  Sec 8     | Environmental Management Systems (ISO 14001) | Critical     | Full Scope
  Sec 9     | Restricted Substances Compliance (MRSL/RSL)  | Critical     | Full Scope
  Sec 10    | Energy Consumption & Carbon Intensity        | Critical     | ACTIVE MVP

  Sec 12    | Air & Noise Emissions Monitoring             | Critical     | Full Scope

2. FIRST-TIME TANNERY ONBOARDING & BASELINE CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

2.1 Navigating to the LWG Module
--------------------------------
1. Log in to the GreenLedger ESG Platform.
2. In the primary left-hand navigation sidebar, locate the "Leather (LWG)" entry
   (identifiable by the golden `workspace_premium` medal icon).
3. Click "Leather (LWG)".
4. If your organization has not yet configured its tannery profile, the system
   Global Profile Guard will automatically direct you to the Onboarding Wizard
   at `/tenant/lwg/onboarding`.

2.2 Facility Linking & Registered Tannery Details
-------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Primary Operational Facility
  - Select your physical operating facility from the dropdown. This links LWG
    auditing data to your existing GreenLedger tenant facility asset.
Step 2: Tannery Registered Business Name
  - Enter the exact registered legal entity name as shown on statutory SPCB/PCC
    environmental consents (e.g., "Evergreen Tannery Pvt Ltd").
Step 3: City and State / Province
  - Enter the manufacturing location (e.g., "Ranipet", "Tamil Nadu").

2.3 Manufacturing Stage Classification & Audit Denominators
-----------------------------------------------------------
Under Section 2 of the onboarding wizard, check all production stages operated:
  [ ] Stage 1: Raw hide to Wet Blue (Beamhouse & Tanning operations)
  [ ] Stage 2: Wet Blue to Crust (Re-tanning, Retanning Drums & Dyeing)
  [ ] Stage 3: Crust to Finished Leather (Coating, Spraying, Milling, Finishing)
  [ ] Stage 1 to 3: Fully Integrated Tannery (Raw hide to finished leather)
  [ ] Stage 2 to 3: Processing from Wet Blue to Finish

*ESG Auditor Note*: You must select at least one stage. The highest stage checked
will automatically serve as the denominator for all energy (MJ/m²) and water
(L/m²) performance metrics.

2.4 Measurement Unit Preference Configuration
---------------------------------------------
Select your organization's primary working leather area unit:
  (•) Square Feet (sq ft) - Traditional commercial trade unit across India/Asia.
  ( ) Square Meters (m²)   - Official international metric unit per LWG standard.
*Note*: Whichever unit you select as primary, the GreenLedger platform auto-converts
every single entry into both units simultaneously in the database.

2.5 Wastewater Treatment & Effluent Disposal Architecture
---------------------------------------------------------
Select your facility's wastewater effluent treatment topology:
  - "Direct discharge with own on-site ETP": Complete primary, secondary, and tertiary
    treatment on-site with direct treated surface discharge.
  - "Pre-treatment with discharge to CETP": On-site primary screening/equalization
    with conveyance to a Common Effluent Treatment Plant cluster.
  - "Both on-site ETP and CETP": Hybrid operations with Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)
    or RO permeate reuse combined with CETP reject discharge.

2.6 Subcontracted Operations & Scope 3 Traceability Boundary
------------------------------------------------------------
Toggle the "Facility uses external subcontracting" switch if your tannery sends
hides off-site for intermediate processing steps (e.g., contract splitting, custom
shaving, embossing, or toll tanning).
*Compliance Impact*: Enabling this flag activates Protocol Section 2 audit rules,
requiring subcontractor audit certifications to prevent certification loss.

2.7 Automated Baseline Initialization & Default Seedings
--------------------------------------------------------
Click "Complete Setup & Launch LWG Workspace".
Upon submission:
  1. The backend automatically calculates your active 24-month sliding window:
     - `data_window_end`: First day of previous calendar month.
     - `data_window_start`: Exactly 23 months prior (24 total months).
  2. The system auto-seeds your default factory departments:
     - Beamhouse (Stage 1)
     - Tan Yard (Stage 1)
     - Re-tanning & Dye house (Stage 2)
     - Finishing Department (Stage 3)
     - Effluent Treatment Plant / RO (Common)
     - Boiler House & Utilities (Common)
  3. You are redirected immediately to the Executive Dashboard (`/tenant/lwg`).

3. FACTORY DEPARTMENTS & METERING SUBUNITS MANAGEMENT
================================================================================
Route: `/tenant/lwg/departments`

3.1 Objectives of Physical Sub-metering under Issue 7.x
-------------------------------------------------------
LWG Protocol Issue 7 awards significant bonus points for facilities that have
isolated electric sub-meters, thermal flow meters, and water meters installed on
individual production lines rather than relying exclusively on a single site-wide
master meter.

3.2 Adding and Categorizing Tannery Departments
-----------------------------------------------
Click "Add Factory Department" to register physical subunits:
  1. Department Name: Enter equipment grouping (e.g., "Finishing Spray Tunnel #3",
     "Chrome Recovery Unit", "Liming Paddles Yard").
  2. Stage Allocation:
     - `stage_1`: Allocated strictly to Beamhouse/Tannery operations.
     - `stage_2`: Allocated strictly to Re-tanning/Dyeing operations.
     - `stage_3`: Allocated strictly to Finishing operations.
     - `common` : Site-wide infrastructure (ETP, Boiler room, Compressor, Admin).
  3. Metering Scope Description: Record meter IDs or physical equipment covered.

3.3 Ongoing Department Lifecycle & Deactivation Rules
-----------------------------------------------------
- To edit an existing subunit, click the edit icon on the department row.
- If a production drum line is decommissioned, do not delete historical records.
  Instead, toggle the "Active Status" switch to Inactive. Inactive departments
  preserve 24-month historical audit records while preventing accidental new
  meter allocations.

  - 24-Month Active Audit Window (e.g., "Audit Window: Nov 2024 – Oct 2026").
  - Default Measurement Unit badge ("Unit: Square Feet (sq ft)").
  - Quick Action Buttons:
    * "Download Auditor Pack": Generates the official multi-sheet Excel file.

  - Tab Switcher: Seamless switching between Dashboard, Production, Energy, Water,
    Evidence Vault, Simulator, Departments, and Excel Tools.

4.2 Panel 1: Audit Readiness Gauge & Stream Completeness
--------------------------------------------------------
Located at the top left:
  - Large Circular Gauge: Visualizes `readiness.overall_percent` (0% to 100%).
    * Emerald (>= 90%) : Audit Ready.
    * Amber (75% - 89%): Action Required (Gaps present).
    * Rose (< 75%)     : Incomplete.
  - Status Badge: Displays "Audit Ready" or "Action Required: Gaps".
  - Three Granular Sub-bars:
    1. Production Completeness (Section 5): % of 24 months with logged throughput.
    2. Energy Completeness (Section 10)  : % of 24 months with logged power/fuels.
    3. Water Completeness (Section 11)   : % of 24 months with logged water intake.

4.3 Panel 2: Specific Energy & Water Intensity KPI Benchmarking
---------------------------------------------------------------
Card 1: Specific Energy Intensity (MJ/m²)
  - Current L12 Value: Specific energy consumption in Megajoules per m² of leather.
  - Baseline P12 Benchmark: Energy rate during the previous 12 months.
  - Trajectory Badge: Year-over-Year (YoY) percentage shift.
    * Green Down-Arrow (e.g., "-9.1% YoY"): Favorable energy efficiency gain.
    * Red Up-Arrow (e.g., "+4.2% YoY")   : Unfavorable energy efficiency loss.

Card 2: Specific Water Intensity (L/m²)
  - Current L12 Value: Specific total water consumption in Litres per m² of leather.
  - Baseline P12 Benchmark: Water rate during the previous 12 months.
  - Trajectory Badge: Year-over-Year (YoY) percentage shift.
    * Green Down-Arrow (e.g., "-8.4% YoY"): Favorable water conservation gain.

4.4 Panel 3: Environmental Shares (Renewable Energy & Water Recycling)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Card 3: Clean Energy & Water Recovery
  - Renewable Energy Share (%): Proportion of total facility energy derived from
    clean sources (On-site rooftop Solar PV, Purchased Renewable PPA, Biomass).
  - Recycled Water Share (%): Percentage of total factory intake sourced from
    treated effluent recycling (own ETP / RO plant or recycled CETP water).

4.5 Panel 4: The 24-Month Audit Data Matrix (Interactive Month Grid)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A visual matrix mapping all 24 months of the audit window:
  - Top Block: Previous 12 Months (P12 - Months 1 to 12).
  - Bottom Block: Latest 12 Months (L12 - Months 13 to 24).
  - Each Month Cell Features 3 Independent Status Dots:
    * Dot 1 [P]: Production Log (Green = Complete, Red = Missing).
    * Dot 2 [E]: Energy Log (Green = Complete, Red = Missing).
    * Dot 3 [W]: Water Log (Green = Complete, Red = Missing).
  - Interactive Cell Flyout: Click any month cell to reveal an instant popover
    displaying recorded figures with direct deep-links to log missing streams.

4.6 Panel 5: Actionable Audit Gaps Scanner & One-Click Fixes
------------------------------------------------------------
Scans your entire 24-month database and statutory permit registry to surface
every non-compliance issue that could cap or fail your audit:
  - Severity Tagging:
    * "Critical Blocker" (Red): Missing monthly operational data, expired SPCB Consent
      to Operate, or missing mandatory borewell meter photo.
    * "Warning" (Amber): SPCB Consent to Operate expiring within 60 days.
  - Section Tag: Points directly to the affected LWG Section (e.g., "Section 11").
  - "Resolve Now" Action Button: Click to navigate directly to the specific form,
    pre-filled with the exact month or section required.

4.7 Panel 6: Predicted Medal Standing & Bottleneck Explainer
------------------------------------------------------------
Displays your projected official LWG Medal:
  - Large Medal Banner: Gold, Silver, Bronze, Audited, or Non-Compliant.
  - Minimum Threshold Score indicator.
  - Holding-Down Bottleneck Alert: If a critical section score restricts the overall
    rating, an alert box identifies the bottleneck section and explains the required

  - Section Rating Pills: Displays individual ratings for tracked critical sections.

4.8 Panel 7: All 17 LWG Protocol Sections Status Table
------------------------------------------------------
A complete breakdown of all 17 LWG protocol sections with quick-filter tabs:
  - "All (17)": Complete issue 7.x protocol.
  - "Critical (13)": The 13 sections governed by the Holding-Down Rule.
  - "MVP Active (4)": The 4 primary data streams actively calculated by the engine.

5. ROUTINE MONTHLY DATA LOGGING (OPERATIONAL DATA STREAMS)
================================================================================

5.1 Screen 3: Monthly Production Throughput Data Entry (Section 5)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Route: `/tenant/lwg/production`

Compliance Objective:
Maintain a contiguous 24-month record of raw material inputs and normalized finished
leather throughput to serve as the denominator for environmental intensities.

Step-by-Step Logging Procedure:
1. Click "Log Month Entry" to open the production drawer.
2. Select Audit Month: Pick from the 24-month dropdown (e.g., "May 2025 (P12)").
3. Select Production Stage: Pick the relevant stage operated (e.g., "Stage 3: Crust to Finished Leather").
4. Select Input Material Classification:
   - `raw_hide_skin`: Raw salted or fresh hides.
   - `wet_blue`     : Chrome tanned wet blue.
   - `wet_white`    : Chrome-free wet white.
   - `crust`        : Dried crust leather ready for finishing.
5. Input Quantity (Tonnes): Enter total mass of material processed (e.g., `12.50`).
6. Leather Output Quantity:
   - Enter output in your preferred unit (e.g., `100,000` sq ft).
   - Real-Time Helper Text: The system immediately calculates and displays the
     metric equivalent: `≈ 9,290.3 m²`.
7. Sold at this Stage (Optional): If leather was sold as crust prior to finishing,
   record the intermediate area dispatched.
8. Notes / Lot Tracking ID: Enter tannery batch or lot reference (e.g., "Lot B-104").
9. Click "Save Production Entry".

*In-Place Upsert Architecture*:
If you select a Month and Stage that has already been recorded, GreenLedger will
seamlessly update the existing entry in place without creating duplicate records.

5.2 Screen 4: Monthly Energy Consumption Logging (Section 10)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Route: `/tenant/lwg/energy`

Compliance Objective:
Record all fossil fuel combustion, utility electricity intake, and on-site clean
generation, converting all values to standardized Gigajoules (GJ).

Step-by-Step Logging Procedure:
1. Click "Log Energy Entry".
2. Select Audit Month: Choose month within the 24-month sliding window.
3. Select Energy Source (9 Taxonomy Classifications):
   - `grid_electricity`                 : Municipal / State electric grid.
   - `diesel_hsd`                        : High Speed Diesel for generators/boilers.
   - `furnace_oil`                       : Heavy Fuel Oil / Boiler furnace oil.
   - `natural_gas_png`                   : Piped Natural Gas.
   - `lpg`                               : Liquefied Petroleum Gas.
   - `coal`                              : Industrial boiler coal.
   - `solar_onsite`                      : On-site rooftop or ground-mount Solar PV.
   - `purchased_renewable_electricity`   : Off-site Power Purchase Agreement (PPA).
   - `biomass_or_briquettes`             : Agricultural waste briquettes / pellets.
4. Select Unit (Strict Dynamic Filtering):
   The unit dropdown dynamically filters based on your selected energy source:
   - Grid / Solar / PPA Renewable : Restricted strictly to `kwh` or `mwh`.
   - Diesel / Furnace Oil         : Restricted strictly to `litres`.
   - Natural Gas PNG              : Restricted strictly to `scm` (standard cubic meters).
   - Coal / Biomass               : Restricted strictly to `tonnes`.
   - LPG                          : Restricted to `kg` or `litres`.
5. Enter Quantity Consumed: Enter the numerical meter consumption.
6. Live Gigajoule (GJ) Preview Calculator:
   Observe the live calculated energy output displayed in amber:
   - Formula: `Quantity * Energy GJ Factor = Live GJ`
   - Example: `10,000 kWh * 0.0036 GJ/kWh = 36.00 GJ`.
7. Subunit Allocation: Assign to a specific factory department or "Whole Site".
8. Mandatory Proof for Purchased Renewable Power:
   - If `purchased_renewable_electricity` is chosen, the upload dropzone becomes
     MANDATORY. You must upload your PPA agreement or monthly green tariff invoice.
9. Click "Save Energy Entry".

5.3 Screen 5: Monthly Water Usage & Balance Logging (Section 11)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Route: `/tenant/lwg/water`

Compliance Objective:
Track fresh water extraction, municipal piped intake, and treated wastewater recycling
to compute facility water balance and water recycling share.

Step-by-Step Logging Procedure:
1. Click "Log Water Intake".
2. Select Audit Month: Choose month within the 24-month sliding window.
3. Select Water Source:
   - Fresh Water Sources    : `borewell`, `municipal_supply`, `tanker`, `rainwater_harvested`
   - Recycled Water Sources : `recycled_own_etp`, `recycled_cetp`
4. Enter Volume Consumed: Enter intake in cubic meters (m³).
   - Note: 1 m³ = 1,000 Litres.
5. Flow Meter Device ID (Mandatory Rule):
   - If `borewell` or `municipal_supply` is selected, Flow Meter ID is MANDATORY.
     Enter the calibrated device serial number (e.g., "BW-METER-01").
6. Borewell Dial Photo Upload (Mandatory Rule):
   - If `borewell` is selected, you MUST upload a photographic image of the water
     flow meter odometer dial with the date/time visible.
7. Subunit Allocation: Assign intake to ETP, Beamhouse, Finishing, or Whole Site.
8. Click "Save Water Entry".

6.4 Mandatory Statutory Expiry Tracking (Section 4 Rules)
---------------------------------------------------------
Per LWG Protocol Section 4 rules, if you select `consent_to_operate` or
`hazardous_waste_authorisation`, the Expiry Date field is STRICTLY MANDATORY.
The system will block submission if an expiry date is omitted.

6.5 The Dynamic Expiry Countdown Alert Engine
---------------------------------------------
The system automatically compares document expiry dates against the current date:
  - Status "valid" (Green Badge):
    Document is active with > 60 days remaining.
  - Status "warning" (Amber Badge):
    `0 <= days_remaining <= 60`. Triggers warning on Dashboard:
    "SPCB Consent to Operate expires on YYYY-MM-DD (within 60 days)."
  - Status "expired" (Red Badge):
    `days_remaining < 0`. Triggers Critical Blocker on Dashboard:
    "SPCB Consent to Operate EXPIRED. LWG accreditation cannot proceed."

6.6 Document Audit Trail, Download Streams, and Purge Rules
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Every vaulted file is assigned a permanent immutable S3 key.
- Auditors can click the download icon to retrieve the original binary document.
- Deleting an evidence record requires two-step confirmation to prevent accidental
  data loss during audits.

7. AUDIT MEDAL SIMULATOR & SCENARIO MODELING (SECTION 9)
================================================================================
Route: `/tenant/lwg/audit-simulation`

Compliance Objective:
Simulate hypothetical protocol scoring scenarios across all 16 scoring sections
(Sections 2 through 17), identify Holding-Down bottlenecks, and formulate
actionable remediation plans before the official auditor arrives on-site.

7.1 Purpose of the Issue 7.x 16-Section Scoring Simulator
---------------------------------------------------------
Section 1 of the protocol covers general company background and carries no score.
The remaining 16 sections (Sections 2 to 17) carry direct percentage scores that
determine your final accreditation tier.

7.2 Interactive Matrix Sliders (Sections 2 through 17)
------------------------------------------------------
The simulator presents 16 responsive slider cards. Each card displays:
  - Section Number and Full Title.
  - Classification: "Critical for Medal" (Shield Icon) vs "Non-Critical".
  - Dual Synchronized Controls:
    * Drag the visual slider from 0% to 100%.
    * Or type the exact percentage score in the numerical input box.
  - Individual Section Tier Badge: Gold (>=85%), Silver (>=75%), Bronze (>=65%),
    Audited (>=50%), Fail (<50%).

7.3 Live Client-Side Medal Evaluation Engine
--------------------------------------------
As you adjust any slider, GreenLedger's client-side calculation engine
(`calculateMedalFromScores`) re-evaluates your overall standing in real time:
  - Calculates lowest score achieved among the 13 Critical Sections.
  - Computes overall projected medal rating (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Audited, Fail).
  - Updates the master medal crest at the top of the screen instantly.

7.4 The Bottleneck Identification & Holding-Down Explainer Box
--------------------------------------------------------------
If any critical section score drags down the facility medal, the system renders a
prominent amber alert box detailing the exact bottleneck:

  "Bottleneck Identified: Critical Section 11 (Water Usage) has score 72.0%
   (Bronze). Even though Sections 4, 5, and 10 achieved Gold (88–92%), your overall
   medal is capped at Bronze. Increase Section 11 score to 75% to unlock SILVER,
   or 85% to unlock GOLD."

This allows ESG directors to target capital investments (e.g., investing in an RO
recycling plant to boost Section 11 from 72% to 86%) to unlock a Gold Medal.

7.5 Simulation Presets
----------------------
Use the top preset buttons to model standardized targets in one click:
  - "All Gold (88%)"   : Sets all 16 sections to 88% to verify Gold requirements.
  - "All Silver (78%)" : Sets all 16 sections to 78% for Silver readiness.
  - "All Bronze (68%)" : Sets all 16 sections to 68% for Bronze baseline.
  - "Audited (50%)"    : Sets all sections to the minimum 50% threshold.
  - "Reset to Saved Audit Scores": Reloads previous official audit results.

7.6 Recording & Archiving Official On-Site Audit Results
--------------------------------------------------------
When your official LWG on-site audit concludes:
1. Adjust all 16 section scores to match the lead auditor's finalized report.
2. Click "Save As Audit Result".
3. Record the official Audit Inspection Date and certificate notes.
4. Click "Confirm & Save".
5. The result is permanently archived in GreenLedger as the facility benchmark.

8. BULK EXCEL INGESTION & AUDITOR DATA PACK GENERATION
================================================================================
Route: `/tenant/lwg/excel-tools`

8.1 Purpose of Bulk Ingestion for 2-Year Historical Records
-----------------------------------------------------------
Entering 24 months of production, energy (multiple fuels), and water data manually
can require over 150 individual form submissions. The Bulk Ingestion Engine allows
you to populate a standardized 3-sheet Excel workbook offline and ingest your entire
2-year historical record in under 5 seconds.

8.2 Step 1: Downloading the Blank 3-Sheet Excel Template
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to `/tenant/lwg/excel-tools`.
2. In Step 1, click "Download 3-Sheet Excel Template (.xlsx)".
3. Open the downloaded workbook `LWG_Bulk_Back_Data_Template.xlsx`.
4. Inspect the three pre-formatted worksheets:
   - Sheet 1 "Production":
     Headers: `month`, `stage`, `input_quantity_tonnes`, `input_material`,
              `output_quantity`, `sold_at_stage`, `notes`.
   - Sheet 2 "Energy":
     Headers: `month`, `source`, `quantity`, `unit`, `department`,
              `bill_proof_file_key`.
   - Sheet 3 "Water":
     Headers: `month`, `source`, `volume_m3`, `meter_id`, `department`,
              `meter_photo_file_key`.

8.3 Step 2: Populating Multi-Sheet Historical Data
--------------------------------------------------
Populate the 24 calendar months (Month 1 to Month 24):
  - Ensure dates follow ISO format: `YYYY-MM-01`.
  - Use exact enum identifiers:
    * Stages   : `stage_1_raw_to_wetblue`, `stage_2_wetblue_to_crust`, `stage_3_crust_to_finish`
    * Materials: `raw_hide_skin`, `wet_blue`, `wet_white`, `crust`
    * Energy   : `grid_electricity`, `diesel_hsd`, `solar_onsite`, `natural_gas_png`
    * Water    : `borewell`, `municipal_supply`, `recycled_own_etp`
  - Ensure energy units match allowed source mappings (`kwh` for grid, `litres` for diesel).

8.4 Step 3: Atomic Workbook Parsing & Ingestion
-----------------------------------------------
1. In Step 2, drag your completed `.xlsx` file into the upload dropzone.
2. Click "Process & Import Workbook".
3. The GreenLedger backend parses all 3 sheets atomically, executes validation
   checks against LWG Issue 7 rules, and upserts all valid records.

8.5 Step 4: Interpreting Row-Level Error Diagnostics & Resolving Issues
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
If any row contains an invalid unit, date outside the 24-month window, or missing
mandatory meter ID, the Ingestion Diagnostic Report displays a structured table:
  - Sheet Name   : Identifies the exact sheet (e.g., "Energy").
  - Row #        : Highlights the exact spreadsheet row (e.g., "Row 14").
  - Target Field : Pinpoints the offending column (e.g., "unit").
  - Reason       : Human-readable diagnostic (e.g., "Unit 'litres' is not allowed for source 'grid_electricity'. Allowed units: kwh, mwh.").

Correct the row in Excel, save, and re-upload. When `total_failed === 0`, your
Dashboard readiness automatically transitions to 100% "Ready".

8.6 Exporting the Official Publication-Ready 6-Sheet Auditor Data Pack
----------------------------------------------------------------------
When preparing for certification audit submission:
1. Navigate to `/tenant/lwg/excel-tools` or click "Download Auditor Pack" in the
   master header.
2. Click "Export Official Auditor Pack (.xlsx)".
3. The platform streams a formatted, publication-ready Excel workbook:
   `LWG_Auditor_Data_Pack_<URN>_<Date>.xlsx`.

The Auditor Data Pack contains 6 comprehensive worksheets:
  - Sheet 1: "Tannery Profile & URN":
    Facility details, operational address, stages run, effluent treatment setup,
    subcontracting scope, and 24-month audit window dates.
  - Sheet 2: "Executive Summary":
    Readiness % gauge, Specific Energy Intensity (L12 vs P12 benchmark in MJ/m²),
    Specific Water Intensity (L12 vs P12 benchmark in L/m²), YoY efficiency gains,
    Renewable Energy Share %, and Recycled Water Share %.
  - Sheet 3: "Production":
    Complete 24-month throughput log with raw material inputs (tonnes) and normalized
    leather surface area in both m² and sq ft.
  - Sheet 4: "Energy":
    Complete 24-month fuel & electricity consumption breakdown normalized to Gigajoules
    (GJ), clean power shares, and PPA proof keys.
  - Sheet 5: "Water":
    Complete 24-month water intake balance, fresh vs recycled ratios, borewell extraction,
    and flow meter register.
  - Sheet 6: "Evidence Vault Index":
    Full inventory of vaulted compliance files, statutory SPCB Consent to Operate,
    Hazardous Waste authorizations, expiry dates, and countdown statuses.
